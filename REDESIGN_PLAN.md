# Bareey Website Redesign Plan

## 1. Site Architecture
- **Home (`/`)**: Main landing page with all 10 sections.
- **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)**: Dedicated page for privacy details.
- **Terms of Service (`/terms`)**: Dedicated page for terms and conditions.
- **App Routes**: Keep existing `/feed`, `/market`, `/stream`, `/chats` but ensure they are accessible via a "Web App" link or similar, while the main landing page focuses on conversion.

## 2. Section Breakdown & Content
### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline**: "The Trusted Marketplace for Your Community."
- **Sub-headline**: "Buy, sell, and access professional services with confidence. Bareey connects you to what you need, securely and simply."
- **CTAs**: [Download on Play Store] (Primary), [Explore Marketplace] (Secondary).
- **Visual**: Android phone mockup showing the Bareey app interface.

### 2. Features Section
- **Buying**: Browse a wide range of products and services from verified sellers.
- **Selling**: List your items or services in minutes and reach your local community.
- **Secure Transactions**: Built-in protection for every payment and exchange.
- **Support Services**: Access Bareey's dedicated support for logistics and help.

### 3. How It Works
- **For Buyers**: Search -> Connect -> Secure Payment -> Receive.
- **For Sellers**: List -> Chat -> Sell -> Get Paid.

### 4. Android App Showcase
- Focus on the mobile-first experience.
- Features: Real-time chats, instant notifications, easy listing management.

### 5. Trust & Credibility
- **Secure Payments**: Encrypted transaction processing.
- **Verified Users**: Building a community of trust.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Dedicated team to help if things don't go as planned.

### 6. FAQ
- Common questions about account safety, payment methods, and service requests.

### 7. About Section
- Bareey's mission to empower local commerce and service providers through technology.

### 8. Contact/Support
- Direct links to support email and help center.

### 9. Footer
- Links to Terms, Privacy, Socials, and App Store.

## 3. Technical Implementation
- **Framework**: SvelteKit (Existing).
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (to be added for modern, rapid UI development) or clean CSS modules.
- **Components**: Create reusable components for Sections, Buttons, and Cards.
- **Assets**: Use Lucide-Svelte for icons (already installed).

## 4. Design Language
- **Colors**: Professional Blue/Black/White palette.
- **Typography**: Inter (already in use) for a clean, modern look.
- **Spacing**: Generous white space for a premium feel.
- **Animations**: Subtle fade-ins and hover effects.
